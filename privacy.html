Privacy Policy

19-0 is a free fan-made game. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and who it's shared with.

Last updated: 17 July 2026.

Who this is

19-0 is run by an individual as a fan project, not a company. George Hughes, trading as 19-0, is the data controller for the information described below. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

What we collect

Playing without an account

You can play the whole game without creating an account. Your progress (high score, run count) is saved locally in your browser using localStorage and never leaves your device.

Creating an account

If you register, we store your username, email address, and a hashed PIN (never your PIN itself) so you can sign back in, resume saved runs, and appear on the leaderboard. This is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

The OddBalls giveaway

If you complete an unbeaten World Cup run, you can optionally enter a prize giveaway run with our partner OddBalls. Entering collects your first name, last name, and contact email, and requires you to confirm you accept the giveaway Terms & Conditions and that you're a UK resident aged 18 or over. If you win, we share your name and email with OddBalls so they can contact you and arrange your prize. We don't share this with anyone else.

Analytics

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data: which screens are visited, how often a run is completed, that kind of thing. No name or email is ever included in this data. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no usage data is collected; the game works identically either way.

Advertising

19-0 shows ads served by Google AdSense to help cover hosting costs. AdSense may set its own cookies and use device identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads based on your activity across other sites, in line with Google's own privacy policy. We don't control what AdSense collects; see Google's policy below for how to opt out of personalised ads.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase: hosts our database (accounts, saved runs, giveaway entries). They process data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.
  • Google: Analytics (usage data, if you accept cookies) and AdSense (ad serving). See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • OddBalls: only if you win the giveaway, so they can contact you and send your prize.

We never sell your data to anyone.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Giveaway entries are kept until the draw is complete and, for the winner, until the prize has been sent; all entries are then deleted within 30 days of the draw.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to how it's used. Email us (below) and we'll action it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you think we've mishandled your data.

Children

19-0 isn't directed at children, and the OddBalls giveaway is restricted to UK residents aged 18+. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Your cookie choices

You can change your cookie preference at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer of the main game.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email george.19-0@outlook.com or find us on X/Twitter. 19-0 is an unofficial fan project.
