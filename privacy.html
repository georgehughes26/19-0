Privacy Policy

19-0 is a free fan-made game. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your data.

What we collect

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, things like which screens are visited and how often a run is completed. This helps us understand how the game is played and improve it.

  • No personal information (name, email, etc.) is ever collected through analytics
  • No data is sold to third parties

If you decline

No analytics cookies are set and no usage data is collected. The game works identically either way.

Your choices

You can change your cookie preference at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer of the main game. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data storage

Game progress (your high score and run count) is saved locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device. If you create a free account, your username and saved runs are stored so you can pick up a run later and appear on the leaderboard.

Contact

Questions? The game is an unofficial fan project, find us on X/Twitter.
