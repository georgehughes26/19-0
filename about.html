About 19-0

A fan-made cricket draft-and-simulation game, built by one fan for cricket fans.

What it is

19-0 lets you build a "dream XI" by spinning through real players from any era and any nation, then live out a full campaign with that squad: matches, form swings, injuries, off-pitch drama, and a season review at the end. It's built for short, one-handed sessions. Spin a squad, pick your XI, simulate the match, see the result.

This isn't a stats spreadsheet. Every screen is built to feel like sports broadcast coverage (scorecards, pundit reaction, breaking-news event screens), because the point is to relive players who never actually shared a pitch, not to crunch numbers.

Three ways to play

T20 Blast

Spin the county. Spin the year. Draft your all-time T20 Blast XI and take it through 16 group games, then Quarter-Final, Semi-Final and Final. Can you go 19-0?

Test Cricket

Draft your all-time England Test XI and compete in the World Test Championship across 6 series. Manage form, injuries and media drama on the way to the WTC Final.

World Cup

Draft an all-time World Cup XI from any nation and era. Play 6 group-stage matches, reach the Super Sixes, then the semi-final and final. One innings a side — every run counts.

Who it's for

Casual, mobile-first cricket fans who want a quick session during downtime: on a commute, at the ground between overs, or on the sofa. The whole loop is designed to work cleanly with one thumb on a phone, no desktop-only fiddling required.

Why it exists

19-0 started as a way to answer the argument every cricket fan has had: could this era's team actually live with that one? Rather than settle it with an opinion, you draft it, simulate it, and find out. A good run leaves you either vindicated or robbed by the result, wanting to run it back.
